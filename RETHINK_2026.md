## The Three Diagnoses (One Per Critic)

### 1. Architecture (cascaded gate + bilinear + CORAL + multi-task)

**Verdict**: structurally wrong supervision × inductive-bias collision.

- Pseudo-labels (text-derived) point at TEXT features.
- Gold labels (audio-anchored laughter) point at AUDIO features.
- Hard multiplicative text-gate *zeros audio when text uncertain* — structurally kills delivery-based humor.
- Mean-pooled WavLM at segment level **destroys temporal info** — laughter is a temporal event.
- 1M params on 12,780 segments = ~110 params/sample → memorizing pseudo-label noise, not learning.

**Specific recommended replacements (literature-grounded)**:
1. Concat + MLP late fusion (not multiplicative gate)
2. Frame-level WavLM-Large with LoRA r=8 on last 2 transformer layers
3. Flip task hierarchy: **laughter detection primary**, humor auxiliary
4. Drop Reddit upvote pretrain — its mechanism is observability bias, not comedy

**Numerical claim**: Tier 1 fixes alone → gold AUC 0.61 → **0.70–0.75**. With Tier 2 (Qwen2-Audio-7B + LoRA), ceiling ~0.80.

### 2. Data & Evaluation

**Pseudo-label paradigm is broken.** Literature (Arazo 2020, Oliver 2018) shows pseudo-labeling fails when noise rate exceeds ~30%. Pseudo-labels were likely generated by the same model family you've been iterating on → **confirmation bias**. The 0.86 pseudo AUC measures consistency with your own labeling process, not predictive power.

**Reddit upvotes ≠ laughter.** Mechanism:
- Reddit upvotes: lexical incongruity, relatability, shareability
- Standup laughter: timing, pause structure, audience contagion, vocal modulation
- **The 634 unlabeled video IDs are useless without annotation** — without gold, they only scale the pseudo-label quality (which is what's hurting us)

**Power analysis on gold AUC 0.59**:
- SE_HanleyMcNeil = √(0.59·0.41/168 + 0.59·0.41/12600) ≈ **0.038**
- **95% CI: [0.516, 0.664]** — could be 0.52 or 0.66. The lower bound is essentially random.
- To distinguish 0.59 vs 0.65 (a 6-pt delta, plausible improvement) at α=0.05, power=0.80: need **400 positives**. We have 168.

**Realistic ceiling without new gold annotations**: **0.65–0.68**. To reach literature SOTA (0.75+) we need 50–200 more gold-annotated standup videos. Period.

**UR-FUNNY (1,866 TED samples)**: high-leverage for **text encoder pretraining only** (transfer likely +0.02–0.04 AUC). Acoustic features are anti-correlated with standup laughter prosody (TED is clean public-speaking; standup is audience-reactive).

### 3. Methodology

**The pattern across versions is the smoking gun**:

| Version | Architecture change | Humor AUC |
|---------|---------------------|-----------|
| v7 | Baseline cascade gate | **0.860** |
| v8 | + Reddit pretrain | 0.802 (−0.058) |

**Net change over 4 iterations and weeks of work: −0.04 AUC, all within noise.**

**Confidence intervals** (Hanley-McNeil, 128 test samples, ~10–20% positive rate):
- v7 0.860 → 95% CI [0.71, 0.98]
- v10 0.8225 → 95% CI [0.68, 0.97]
- **All four versions are statistically indistinguishable.**

**The laugh auxiliary task at 0.16% positive rate**:
- Training set has ~1 positive example (0.8 per fold at 80/20 split)
- SNR ≈ √(N_pos/N_neg) = √0.0016 = 0.04
- 30% of total gradient direction is dominated by this noise
- **Trivially solvable by predicting P(laugh)=0 for everything.** No learning happens.
